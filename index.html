Ordering is simple

Everything starts with a message.

  1. Send your details

    Message us on WhatsApp or email with the event type, a description, a photo of your cake idea, the flavour and the date you need it.

  2. Secure your date

    A 50% non-refundable deposit confirms your order. Standard orders need 3 days’ notice, custom orders 7 days.

  3. Collect or get it delivered

    Pick up your order, or have it delivered for a delivery fee.
