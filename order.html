How to order

Fill in the form and it opens a ready-written message on WhatsApp or email. Add a photo of your cake idea once the chat opens.

Your order details

Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp or your email app. Attach your reference photo there.

Ts and Cs

  • Standard orders need to be placed 3 days prior to collection date.
  • Custom orders need to be placed 7 days prior to collection date.
  • A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your order.
  • A delivery fee is charged. Pick-up is available.
  • An additional charge applies to express orders (24 hours prior to the event).
